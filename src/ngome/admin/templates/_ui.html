How it’s counted

  • Counts are of installs, not people: a reinstall or a second phone counts twice. Phones with counting off are not in them.
  • Any count under 10 shows as <10. When only one cell is hidden, the next smallest shows as hidden, so the total cannot give it away. Sums beside a table follow the same rule.
  • One dimension at a time: version, build, or (by month only) country. Never country by version or build.
  • Crash reports are sent only when the person says yes, built from an allow-list of fields, kept 90 days, and not linked to the monthly count.
  • No per-person data: no ids, addresses, accounts or times of day are stored or shown.
